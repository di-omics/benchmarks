How each capability is evaluated

Measured,
not asserted.

Each repo is checked on two axes: whether the science holds and whether it runs unattended. Physical execution is measured with Rhodamine B, Qubit, and computer vision, never inferred from a software log.

See the method Per-capability status
01

Two axes

Axis 1 · Science

Does the science hold?

The physical biology and chemistry executed correctly, and the bioinformatics recovers ground truth, against a metric fixed before the run.

Rules out: passing in silico while the wet step silently failed. Physical execution is verified, never assumed.
Axis 2 · Autonomy

Does it run unattended?

Physical-AI QC in the loop, error handling that fails closed, and closed-loop feedback where the agent reads QC, corrects, and re-runs.

Rules out: a demo that only works with a human quietly saving it. The loop has to close on its own.
02

The infrastructure behind the checks

Liquid-handling QC
Verified
Rhodamine B for per-well CV%, Qubit or PicoGreen for yield. Upfront, fail-closed.
Computer-vision QC
Verified
Reads wells, pipetting, and steps from bench video. Ground-truth-validated.
Error handling
Verified
Guards, do-not-over-dry limits, one approval before purchase. Fails closed on readiness.
Robust bioinformatics
Verified
Reproducible fastq-to-result pipelines: scanpy, BJ-WGS Nextflow, versioned.
Preset statistics
Verified
Metric and threshold fixed before the run. Plant ground truth, recover, score.
Closed-loop agentic feedback
Building
Agent reads QC and CV, corrects the deck, and re-runs without a human in the path.
03

Guardrails against fooling ourselves

  • Threshold before the run. The metric and the pass line are fixed up front, never chosen after the numbers land.
  • Physical execution is measured, not trusted. Rhodamine B, Qubit, and computer vision confirm the wet step actually happened correctly.
  • In silico alone does not clear the science axis. A recovery score on synthetic data is a start, not a pass.
